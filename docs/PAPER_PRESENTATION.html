🧭 First-Time Reader Primer: What Is This Project?

If you have never heard about this project before, this section provides an intuitive, non-jargon orientation explaining the problem, the core difficulty, and our solution.

A

What is the Problem?

The Dead Sea Scrolls (2,000-year-old Jewish manuscripts) are damaged by rot and tearing. Editors must fill missing gaps ("lacunae"). Manual scholar restoration takes decades and is prone to subjective bias.

B

Why Do Standard Masked Language Models (MLMs) Fail (<10% Accuracy)?

Standard Sub-Word MLMs (like MsBERT) predict BPE/WordPiece sub-words. They ignore physical hole sizes and cannot read partial letter ink traces surviving on parchment edges.

C

What Is Our Solution (66.2% Accuracy)?

We built the first scroll-disjoint framework that conditions character-level language models directly on surviving ink traces (e.g. סר⬚⬚ך → סרכיך), more than doubling human scholar retrieval rates.

🚀 Executive Summary & Headline Discoveries

Restoring damaged ancient manuscripts has traditionally been treated either as unconstrained text generation or manual epigraphic guesswork. Our unified framework establishes the first scroll-disjoint baseline conditioning character language models directly on physical ink traces.

"Restoring damaged Dead Sea Scroll lacunae is usually framed as a pure AI text generation task. But unconstrained models get real lacunae right less than 10% of the time. Core Takeaways

  • Physical Evidence Dominance: Partial letter ink traces (סר⬚⬚ך) provide massive signal, boosting Top-10 real lacuna restoration from 9.46% to 66.22%.
  • Primary Baseline Model (TavBERT FT-Optimal): With validation early-stopping, TavBERT leads all benchmarks (23.65% Hit@10 cloze, 47.30% Top-1 real lacunae).
  • The Scoring Trap Exposed: Aligned-only scoring inflates WordPiece models (MsBERT 21.56%), but under fair all-words scoring (unaligned = miss), MsBERT drops to 13.31% due to 38.3% unaligned predictions.
  • Multi-Word Solution: LengthEnsembleCharMLMGenerator solves unknown-length multi-word lacunae (14.2% Hit@10 vs 0.0% single-length wall).

🗣️ Advisor Meeting Q&A Defense

Click any card to review bulletproof answers for tough advisor questions during your presentation:

Q1: "Why do we mask 30% of words instead of standard 15%?" ▼
"Masking 15% of isolated words in clean text is a toy setup that never occurs on real manuscripts. Our audit of 12,971 damaged scroll words shows physical damage accounts for 25%–35% of tokens. 30% masking (scatter-30) mirrors real scroll decay and tests multi-gap context degradation."
Q2: "Why is 23.65% Hit@10 strong for 1-word cloze if length is known?" ▼
"In scatter-30, 30% of the ENTIRE sentence is missing simultaneously. Furthermore, ancient Hebrew prefixes and suffixes create dozens of valid synonyms for a 5-letter slot (e.g. ויאמר vs ויקרא). Exact-match cloze requires predicting the exact 1 gold word out of dozens of valid options. Length alone boosts accuracy 4.4× (5.3% → 23.6%), and adding partial letters pushes it to 66.2%."
Q3: "How do we prove our model didn't cheat by reading scholar guesses?" ▼
"We built an automated Zero-Leak Redaction Engine. In Text-Fabric, all editorial reconstructions are tagged rec=1. Our pipeline 100% redacts all rec=1 signs into blank wildcards (⬚). The model conditions strictly on verified physical ink (rec=0)."
Q4: "How do we get the Human Scholar Baseline (20.3% Top-1 / 43.2% Top-10)?" ▼
"On the 74 Qumran-Digital targets, we evaluated initial preliminary DJD edition readings against final Qimron collated restorations. Initial human scholar readings match final restoration 20.3% Top-1. TavBERT FT-Optimal reaches 47.30% Top-1—more than doubling first-pass human retrieval rates."

📐 System Architecture & Pipeline Design

1

Scroll-Disjoint Dataset Partitioning

732 non-biblical scrolls partitioned via SHA-1 hashes (531 train / 108 val / 93 test) with 0 straddling scrolls.

2

Zero-Leak Epigraphic Redaction Engine

Filters all signs with rec=1 (editorial guesses) into blank wildcards (⬚), keeping only rec=0 physical ink.

3

Autoregressive Partial-Letters Beam Search

Condition character-level probability distribution P(c_i | c_<i) at step i to zero out letters inconsistent with ink trace P[i].

4

Length-Ensemble Generator for Multi-Word Lacunae

Evaluate candidate gap lengths L in [3, 15] with length-penalty log-probability scoring Score = Sum log P / L^0.5.
