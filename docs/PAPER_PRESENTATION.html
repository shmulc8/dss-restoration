What is supported

  • Character tokenization prevents alignment drops.
  • Encoded traces and approximate extent sharply narrow candidates in the selected QD diagnostic.
  • The preserved-only word-span decoder is the simplest current baseline.
  • All paper-facing counts and scores come from frozen artifacts.

What is not supported

  • Historical restoration accuracy or recovery of physical truth.
  • A solved unknown-length multiword task.
  • Model superiority over Embible.
  • A retrieval gain on the 74 QD targets.
  • Any human-participant study.

Remaining promotion gates

  1. Train and evaluate three checkpoints with identical hyperparameters.
  2. Freeze one authoritative split registry before new training.
  3. Complete the formulaic and near-duplicate cross-split audit.
